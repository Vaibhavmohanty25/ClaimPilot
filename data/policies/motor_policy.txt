MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY

SECTION 1 - POLICY PERIOD

Coverage applies only when the incident occurs during the active policy period.

SECTION 2 - OWN DAMAGE COVERAGE

The insurer will indemnify the insured for accidental loss or damage to the insured vehicle arising from collision, overturning, impact, fire, explosion, theft, malicious acts, natural disasters, or other insured perils.

SECTION 3 - COLLISION DAMAGE

Damage caused by accidental collision with another vehicle, object, road barrier, or fixed structure is covered under Own Damage Coverage, subject to the applicable deductible and policy conditions.

SECTION 4 - EXCLUSIONS

The insurer will not be liable for:

1. Damage caused while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
2. Damage caused while the vehicle is being driven without a valid driving licence.
3. Mechanical or electrical breakdown not resulting from an insured event.
4. Consequential loss.
5. Normal wear and tear.
6. Damage arising from racing, speed testing, or illegal use of the vehicle.
7. Fraudulent or intentionally misrepresented claims.

SECTION 5 - DEDUCTIBLE

A compulsory deductible of INR 5,000 applies to each approved Own Damage claim unless otherwise specified in the policy schedule.

SECTION 6 - CLAIM DOCUMENTATION

The insured must provide sufficient evidence of the loss.

Required documentation may include:

- Completed claim form
- Vehicle registration certificate
- Valid driving licence
- Repair estimate
- Photographs of damage
- Police report where applicable
- Any other supporting evidence requested by the insurer

SECTION 7 - CLAIM ASSESSMENT

The insurer may review repair estimates and supporting evidence to determine whether claimed repair items are directly related to the insured event.

Repair items not supported by the reported incident, available photographs, inspection report, police report, or other submitted evidence may be excluded from the payable claim amount.

SECTION 8 - MISREPRESENTATION

If material information relating to the cause, timing, nature, or extent of the loss is intentionally misrepresented, the insurer may investigate the claim further and may reject the claim in accordance with applicable policy terms and law.
